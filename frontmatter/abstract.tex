This project presents the development of a mechatronic system designed to address a specific engineering problem through the balanced integration of the four foundational areas of the discipline: mechanics, electronics, control, and programming. The document describes the mathematical modeling and simulation methodology, the hardware and software architectural design, the integration of sensors, actuators, and digital processing stages, as well as the testing protocol and quantitative metrics employed to experimentally validate the prototype performance under representative operating conditions. The expected results demonstrate the technical feasibility of the proposed solution and its compliance with the specified design requirements.

\vspace{1.5em}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} Automatic control, dynamic modeling, experimental validation, mechatronic development, mechatronics engineering, system design.
