\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

In this project, an autonomous mobile rover robot will be designed and implemented to explore rough terrains, navigate without reliance on satellite positioning systems, identify simulated geological samples, collect, transport, and deposit them in a designated zone, while supervising mission progress via a human-machine interface. A comprehensive mechatronic architecture will be developed, integrating an all-terrain locomotion system capable of negotiating slopes and surface irregularities, electronic power stages, inertial and visual sensing, relative localization and obstacle avoidance algorithms, as well as an automated sample gathering and releasing mechanism. The robotic system performance will be experimentally validated in a controlled terrestrial environment using quantitative metrics for mobility, relative navigation accuracy, sample collection success rate, and overall mission fulfillment percentage.

\vspace{1.5em}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} Autonomous exploration, mobile robotics, non-GPS navigation, rough terrain, rover robot, sample collection.
