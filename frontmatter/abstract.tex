This research protocol focuses on the design and development of an autonomous mobile robot capable of transporting objects weighing up to 1 kg within an indoor environment. The proposal arises from the need for a more accessible alternative to commercial autonomous robots while avoiding dependence on predefined routes. The user will be able to specify the destination to which the object should be delivered through an interface, and the robot will autonomously navigate to the selected location. The development of the robot will integrate different areas of mechatronics, working together to enable the system to move, perceive its surroundings, make decisions, and respond to conditions encountered along its route. Based on the available resources and the proposed specifications, the project aims to develop the robot as an alternative for the autonomous transportation of objects in indoor environments under controlled conditions.

\vspace{1.5em}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} Autonomous mobile robot, autonomous navigation, object transportation, service robot, mobile robotics.
