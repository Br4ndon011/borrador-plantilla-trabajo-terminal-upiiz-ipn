\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

In this project, a state-space mechatronic control system will be designed and implemented for the regulation of longitudinal speed and steering angle in a 1:10 scale ground vehicle. The coupled traction and steering dynamics will be analytically and experimentally modeled, deriving a matrix state-space representation to formally assess system controllability and observability properties. Furthermore, a comprehensive mechatronic architecture will be integrated, comprising speed and angular position sensors, electronic power stages, a steering servomotor, a direct-current traction motor, and a digital processing unit for real-time periodic execution of the state-feedback control law. Finally, the system performance will be quantitatively evaluated under reference step changes, load disturbances, and power supply voltage drops on controlled surfaces, aiming to minimize steady-state speed errors and stabilize transient orientation responses within the specified design tolerances.

\vspace{1.5em}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} 1:10 scale vehicle, mechatronic integration, speed regulation, state-space control, steering regulation, vehicular dynamics.
